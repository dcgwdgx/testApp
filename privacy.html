Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Pet Portrait AI does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Photos you upload are temporarily processed to generate artistic portraits and are not stored on our servers. Uploaded images are sent directly to our AI service provider (Volcengine) solely for the purpose of image generation via the Doubao Seedream API.

2. How We Use Your Photos

Your uploaded pet photos are used exclusively to generate AI-powered artistic portraits. Images are transmitted securely and are not used for any other purpose, including model training, advertising, or analytics. Generated portraits are saved locally on your device only.

3. Data Storage

All generated images and history are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not upload or store your photos or generated portraits on any external servers. You can delete your history at any time from the History screen within the app.

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses the Volcengine (火山引擎) API for AI image generation via the Doubao Seedream model. Uploaded photos are transmitted to Volcengine's servers for processing. Please refer to Volcengine's privacy policy for information on how they handle data during the image generation process.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
